# Business Glossary

Owned by Finance & Analytics. These are the company's official definitions for reporting. All money is
reported in US dollars, converted at the exchange rate on the day of the transaction. "Orders" never
include test orders placed by staff.

## Sales

**Gross revenue**: The value of merchandise sold, after discounts. Excludes shipping charges and sales tax.
Cancelled orders never count toward revenue.

**Refunds**: Money returned to customers for merchandise. When a refund also returned shipping or tax,
only the merchandise portion counts. A refund can never exceed the merchandise value of its order.

**Net revenue**: Gross revenue minus refunds. This is the company's headline revenue number. When someone
says "revenue" without qualification, they mean net revenue.

**Average order value (AOV)**: Net revenue divided by the number of orders, excluding cancelled orders.

**Active customers**: The number of distinct customers who placed at least one order that was not
cancelled during the period being reported.

**First order**: A customer's earliest order.

**Units sold**: Number of items on orders that were not cancelled.

## Membership (subscriptions)

**MRR (monthly recurring revenue)**: Measured at the end of a month. The sum of the monthly price of every
membership that is still active on the last day of that month. Annual plans contribute one twelfth of
their yearly price. A membership canceled during the month contributes nothing for that month.
Memberships with a failed payment ("past due") are still active.

**Active memberships**: Memberships still active on the last day of the month.

**New memberships**: Memberships that started during the month.

**Churned memberships**: Memberships canceled during the month.

## Customer support

**First response time**: The time from when a customer opens a ticket until a support agent first replies
to the customer. Internal notes between agents are not replies. Reported in hours. Tickets with no reply
yet are left out of averages.

**Resolution time**: The time from ticket creation until it was solved, in hours. Only solved tickets count.

**Solved tickets**: Tickets in a solved or closed state.

## Marketing

**Ad spend**: What we paid the ad platforms (Google Ads, Facebook Ads), in US dollars.

**Cost per click (CPC)**: Ad spend divided by clicks for the same period and scope.

**Click-through rate (CTR)**: Clicks divided by impressions.

## Inventory

**Units available**: Stock on hand minus stock already reserved for open orders, as of the end of a
given day. Inventory is a balance, not a flow: report it for a single day, and never add balances
from different days together. "End of the month" means the last day of that month.

## Customers

**Customer segment**: Every customer falls in exactly one segment, checked in this order:
- *Wholesale*: B2B accounts flagged as wholesale in the store.
- *Member*: has ever held a membership.
- *Retail*: everyone else.
