# Business glossary

The company's standard definitions, in plain terms. Amounts are in USD. Unless a term says otherwise, cancelled
orders never count.

## Sales
- **Gross revenue**: merchandise subtotal after discounts. Before refunds; no shipping or tax.
- **Refunds**: refunded merchandise value per order, capped at that order's subtotal.
- **Net revenue**: gross revenue minus refunds. The company's standard revenue metric; "revenue" means this.
- **Orders**: every order, including cancelled ones. **Valid orders** exclude cancelled ones.
- **Average order value (AOV)**: net revenue per valid order.
- **Active customers**: distinct customers with at least one valid order in the period.
- **Units sold**: units on valid orders.
- **Product revenue**: order-line amounts after discounts on valid orders, before refunds.
- **First / repeat order**: a customer's orders in the order they were placed; the first is their first order.

## Membership
- **MRR (monthly recurring revenue)**: at a month end, the sum of monthly prices (annual plans divided by 12) of
  memberships started before then and not cancelled by the last day of the month. Point-in-time: never sum it
  across months.
- **Active subscriptions**: memberships active at month end, counted the same way as MRR.
- **New subscriptions**: memberships started in the period.
- **Churn / churned subscriptions**: memberships cancelled in the period.

## Support
- **First response time**: time from ticket creation to the first *public* agent reply. Internal notes don't count;
  averages cover tickets with a reply.
- **Resolution time**: time from creation to solved, over solved tickets.
- **Solved tickets**: tickets in solved or closed status.

## Marketing
- **Ad spend**: advertising cost. **Cost per click (CPC)**: ad spend / clicks. **Click-through rate (CTR)**:
  clicks / impressions.

## Inventory
- **Units on hand**: units physically in stock, from end-of-day snapshots. Point-in-time: use one snapshot date,
  never sum across dates.
- **Units available to sell**: on hand minus reserved.
